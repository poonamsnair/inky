# Requirements

## Story

- A tiny ant crawls from the left across a garden bed.
- The garden is calm at first: sprouting leaves, stones, soil clumps, paper plant markers, and warm watercolor texture.
- A shovel descends from above, its shadow arriving before the blade.
- The shovel lands with a dirt puff near the ant.
- The ant turns and runs away quickly, with short local motion ticks.

## Style

- Warm off-white textured paper.
- Loose black ink outlines with pressure variation and small wobbles.
- Light watercolor fills in greens, ochres, blue-gray shadows, and terracotta accents.
- Background garden notes should echo the reference image's pinned-paper / research-wall rhythm, but as plant labels and seed packet scraps.
- Avoid polished vector smoothness; lines should feel hand-drawn and slightly imperfect.

## Quality

- Ant must read clearly at thumbnail size: head, thorax, abdomen, six legs, antennae.
- Shovel must read as a wooden handle plus metal blade.
- Impact should not cover the ant; the ant's escape path should be readable.
- Reference image must not appear in final frames.
