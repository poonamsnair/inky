# storyboard In-Between Plan

This plan turns key storyboard panels into a smoother handmade animation. Use the source frames as lighthouses and redraw bridge frames; do not rely on crossfades alone.

## Timing

- FPS: 12
- Key-frame hold: 2 frame(s)
- Base in-betweens per transition: 6
- Total video frames: 90
- Estimated duration: 7.50 seconds
- Loop final scene to first scene: no

## Layer Locking

- Locked set layers: paper grain, panel border, wall notes/posters, table/desk, static background lighting
- Anchored props/layers: laptop, mug, notebooks, papers, phone, lamp, plant, recurring desk props
- Animated layers: head, torso, shoulders, arms, wrists, hands, facial expression, hair droop, dream/sleep/motion marks

## Transition Schedule

| Transition | In-betweens | Easing | Action Change | Clarity Risks |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 -> 2 | 6 | ease-in-out | Woman reads alone on sofa -> Cat appears at right edge | Hands hidden by book; crossed legs disconnect; Cat too small or cut off |
| 2 -> 3 | 6 | ease-out | Cat appears at right edge -> Cat walks left toward sofa | Cat too small or cut off; Walking paws float |
| 3 -> 4 | 6 | settle | Cat walks left toward sofa -> Cat stops and looks up | Walking paws float; Cat gaze unclear |
| 4 -> 5 | 6 | ease-in-out | Cat stops and looks up -> Cat passes in front of sofa | Cat gaze unclear; Cat overlaps shoe/sofa confusingly |
| 5 -> 6 | 6 | ease-out | Cat passes in front of sofa -> Cat crouches to jump | Cat overlaps shoe/sofa confusingly; Crouch reads as lying down |
| 6 -> 7 | 6 | settle | Cat crouches to jump -> Cat jumps onto sofa | Crouch reads as lying down; Do not draw long jump guide arcs as final lines |
| 7 -> 8 | 6 | ease-in-out | Cat jumps onto sofa -> Cat sits beside woman | Do not draw long jump guide arcs as final lines; Cat floats on cushion |
| 8 -> 9 | 6 | ease-out | Cat sits beside woman -> Woman notices cat | Cat floats on cushion; Face hidden or book floats |
| 9 -> 10 | 6 | settle | Woman notices cat -> Cat steps across sofa toward lap | Face hidden or book floats; Cat merges with trousers |
| 10 -> 11 | 6 | ease-in-out | Cat steps across sofa toward lap -> Cat curls on lap | Cat merges with trousers; Cat body becomes blob; lap contact unclear |
| 11 -> 12 | 6 | ease-out | Cat curls on lap -> Final calm reading cuddle | Cat body becomes blob; lap contact unclear; Missing cat face/tail |

## Drawing Notes

- Reuse locked set layers during bridge frames so posters, walls, table, and static lighting do not swim.
- Keep anchored props in stable positions unless the story says they move.
- Interpolate animated anchors first: head, torso, shoulders, forearms, wrists, hands, expression, and story-critical marks.
- Keep face details attached to the head.
- Keep hands attached through wrist, forearm, and sleeve.
- Keep props attached to the desk or hand.
- Redraw ink and watercolor texture mainly on moving forms so the result feels handmade without making the whole set jitter.
- Use motion marks only after body attachment is clear.
