# cat-yarn-watercolor In-Between Plan

This plan turns key storyboard panels into a smoother handmade animation. Use the source frames as lighthouses and redraw bridge frames; do not rely on crossfades alone.

## Timing

- FPS: 12
- Key-frame hold: 2 frame(s)
- Base in-betweens per transition: 6
- Total video frames: 90
- Estimated duration: 7.50 seconds
- Loop final scene to first scene: no

## Layer Locking

- Locked set layers: paper grain, background wash, floor line, lighting
- Anchored props/layers: yarn ball, yarn trail, cast shadow
- Animated layers: cat head, ears, spine, tail, legs, paws, eyes, whiskers, yarn loops when pulled

## Transition Schedule

| Transition | In-betweens | Easing | Action Change | Clarity Risks |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 -> 2 | 6 | ease-in-out | Cat sits upright noticing yarn at right -> Cat crouches and approaches | Yarn too small; cat face unreadable; Floating paws, drifting yarn |
| 2 -> 3 | 6 | ease-out | Cat crouches and approaches -> Cat reaches one gentle paw | Floating paws, drifting yarn; Paw missing contact |
| 3 -> 4 | 6 | settle | Cat reaches one gentle paw -> Cat sniffs close to ball | Paw missing contact; Nose/yarn overlap confusion |
| 4 -> 5 | 6 | ease-in-out | Cat sniffs close to ball -> Cat sits and bats yarn | Nose/yarn overlap confusion; Paw detached, ball pops |
| 5 -> 6 | 6 | ease-out | Cat sits and bats yarn -> Cat leaps/chases rolling ball | Paw detached, ball pops; Cat too abstract in leap |
| 6 -> 7 | 6 | settle | Cat leaps/chases rolling ball -> Cat catches ball low | Cat too abstract in leap; Yarn hidden by cat |
| 7 -> 8 | 6 | ease-in-out | Cat catches ball low -> Cat hugs/holds ball | Yarn hidden by cat; Ball reads as body patch |
| 8 -> 9 | 6 | ease-out | Cat hugs/holds ball -> Cat rolls tangled | Ball reads as body patch; Loops confuse limbs |
| 9 -> 10 | 6 | settle | Cat rolls tangled -> Cat sits tangled | Loops confuse limbs; Loops detach or obscure face |
| 10 -> 11 | 6 | ease-in-out | Cat sits tangled -> Cat pauses tangled | Loops detach or obscure face; Face/harness confusion |
| 11 -> 12 | 6 | ease-out | Cat pauses tangled -> Cat walks away trailing yarn | Face/harness confusion; Missing yarn trail, floating tail |

## Drawing Notes

- Reuse locked set layers during bridge frames so posters, walls, table, and static lighting do not swim.
- Keep anchored props in stable positions unless the story says they move.
- Interpolate animated anchors first: head, torso, shoulders, forearms, wrists, hands, expression, and story-critical marks.
- Keep face details attached to the head.
- Keep hands attached through wrist, forearm, and sleeve.
- Keep props attached to the desk or hand.
- Redraw ink and watercolor texture mainly on moving forms so the result feels handmade without making the whole set jitter.
- Use motion marks only after body attachment is clear.
