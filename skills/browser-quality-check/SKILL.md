# Browser Quality Check

## Checkpoints

- The reference is not visible or copied as pixels.
- Changed objects feel native to the artwork.
- Head, hands, tools, and foreground objects are layered correctly.
- Contours are strong enough to read.
- Pen strokes are dense enough to feel handmade.
- Watercolor areas are soft but not flat.
- Mobile and desktop framing keep the artwork visible.
- Animation does not freeze the browser.
- Any reported visual bug has been fixed by rebuilding the source drawing/path/layer, not by covering it.

## Failure Signs

- Sticker-like objects.
- Redundant old-storyboard content.
- Smooth flat fills with no hatching.
- Head/body or hand/tool layering mistakes.
- The new object looks traced from a different image.
- The final result reads as a patched original rather than a new drawing.
- Fixes leave visible seams, plugs, rings, masks, or mismatched texture.
- A bug is hidden by a cover-up shape instead of corrected in the underlying drawing.
