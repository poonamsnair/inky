# Code Of Conduct

Inky should be a kind, useful place to collaborate.

## Our Standard

Please:

- be respectful and patient
- assume good intent
- welcome people with different experience levels
- give specific, constructive feedback
- keep discussions focused on the work

Please do not:

- harass, insult, or demean others
- use sexualized, discriminatory, or exclusionary language
- share private information without permission
- derail issues or pull requests with unrelated arguments

## Reporting

If something feels unsafe or inappropriate, contact the project maintainers through the repository owner or a private channel listed by the maintainers.

Reports will be handled with care. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other action needed to keep the project healthy.
